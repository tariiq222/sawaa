### F3 history: the orphan deletion did **not** survive a turbo cache-hit build (this is why t10 was needed)

Three independent parts, all re-runnable:

1. t4 verified the two orphan directories deleted and **not** regenerated by its own direct `tsc -p tsconfig.json` build (task completed 00:59:17).
2. The same files were then created on disk at `birth = 2026-09-22 01:05:29` — `stat -f 'birth=%SB modify=%Sm %N' -t '%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S' packages/shared/dist/terminology/index.js packages/shared/dist/date/timezone.js` — i.e. after that deletion, during t5's `pnpm build`.
3. Mechanism, observed rather than inferred: `/tmp/t5-logs/typecheck-build.log:67` shows `@sawaa/shared:build: cache hit, replaying logs e9892ae7f85ab03a`, and `.turbo/cache/e9892ae7f85ab03a-manifest.json` lists 119 captured outputs, **including the four paths by name**: `packages/shared/dist/terminology/index.{js,d.ts}` and `packages/shared/dist/date/timezone.{js,d.ts}`. Root cause: `turbo.json` sets `build.outputs = ["dist/**", ".next/**", "!.next/cache/**"]`, so a cache entry captured before the deletion replays the whole `dist/` tree.

Fallout measured on disk: **38 of 850** `.turbo/cache/*-manifest.json` entries contain outputs of removed sources (`dist/terminology`, `dist/date`, `dist/constants/feature-keys`, `dist/constants/feature-catalog`); the t7 reviewer counted **37** on an independent pass. Both counts are reproducible and the exact figure is not load-bearing; the class of risk is. `packages/shared/dist` is untracked (`git ls-files packages/shared/dist` → 0 paths), so none of this appears in `git status`.

`dist` is build output, and no consumer resolves the two orphans (no source directory, no `exports` mapping, no importing code, zero consumer grep), so this is **not a product defect and is not reopened as a new requirement**. It is recorded as (a) a durability limit of this round's cleanup and (b) one root-cause item for the owner: invalidate/purge the affected cache entries (the four named outputs, and the 37–38 manifests of removed sources) or rebuild shared with a forced clean entry, then re-delete the two literal paths. The independent Round 2 review of Docker context and artifacts closed as `verdict=pass` **after explicitly revising its own criterion wording on this point**: "exports intact after a rebuild" holds, "orphan removed" is **not** declared.
